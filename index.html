I conduct research in insurance economics, risk management, and financial regulation. I have particular interests in capital allocation methodology and market behavior under financial frictions, with a focus on the institutional context of the insurance industry. I have previously served as an economist in the Capital Markets Function of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and as a commercial lines actuary at Fireman's Fund Insurance Companies. I am a past President of the American Risk and Insurance Association (ARIA) and of the Risk Theory Society.

In addition to my academic work, I provide consulting and expert testimony for insurance litigation and rate proceedings.
