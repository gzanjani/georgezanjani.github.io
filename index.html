I conduct research in insurance economics, risk management, and financial regulation. In particular, my work examines capital allocation methodology and market behavior under financial frictions. I have previously served as an economist at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and as an actuary at Fireman's Fund Insurance Companies. I am a past President of the American Risk and Insurance Association (ARIA) and the Risk Theory Society.

In addition to my academic research, I provide consulting and expert testimony for insurance litigation and rate proceedings.
